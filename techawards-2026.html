EPF Tech Awards 2026

The 4th Annual EPF Tech Awards — celebrating Africa's boldest innovators, founders, and changemakers shaping the continent's digital future.

Award Categories

Your innovation deserves recognition. The EPF Tech Awards is now accepting nominations across 8 categories.

Tech Visionary of the Year

For leaders and thinkers who are shaping the strategic direction of technology in South Africa and across the continent.

Startup of the Year

Recognising the most promising early-stage venture turning a bold idea into a scalable, real-world technology solution.

Social Impact Award

For innovators using technology to solve critical social challenges, from education and healthcare to water, energy, and employment.

AI & Data Excellence of the Year

Honours the most impactful application of artificial intelligence and data science deployed to improve lives across Africa.

FinTech Innovator Award

Celebrating breakthroughs in financial technology, from digital payments and lending to insurance and financial inclusion tools.

Women in Tech Award

Celebrating women who are breaking barriers, building tech businesses, and inspiring the next generation of female innovators across Africa.

TownshipTech Leader

Recognising township-based innovators and entrepreneurs building technology solutions that transform their communities from within.

People's Choice Tech Award

Voted by the public, for the innovator or team whose work has resonated most with communities across South Africa.

3rd Annual EPF Tech Awards: Recap

A Night That Celebrated Africa's Best

The 3rd Annual EPF Tech Awards brought together South Africa's most inspiring innovators, startups, and tech leaders for a night of recognition, inspiration, and community. Watch the highlights from last year's ceremony.

The 4th Annual EPF Tech Awards is coming on 12 September 2026.

Nominations are open now. If last year's winners inspired you, this is your year to step forward. Whether you're an AI builder, a fintech founder, a township tech leader, or a woman changing the game. Your work deserves to be on that stage.

How Nominations Work

01
Submit a Nomination

Nominate yourself or someone you know via our online form. Nominations are open to individuals and organisations across Africa.

02
Panel Review

A panel of industry experts, EPF advisors, and past award winners shortlist nominees based on impact, innovation, and scalability.

03
Finalist Announcement

Finalists are announced publicly and invited to present at the EPF Tech Awards ceremony.

04
Awards Night

Winners are announced at the annual EPF Tech Awards gala, a celebration of Africa's brightest tech talent.
