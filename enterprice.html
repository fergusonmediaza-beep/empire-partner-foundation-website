OUR PROGRAMMES

Three Programmes. One Ecosystem.

EPF runs three integrated programmes — each targeting a different stage of economic development, sharing one goal: sustainable, measurable impact across South Africa.

Select a programme to explore

Venture Builder

Bridge the Gap from Startup Fragmentation to Market Scale.

Early-stage tech startups face highly fragmented funding systems, struggle to land their first enterprise customer, and lack market-demand-led innovation.

The EPF Venture Builder actively intervenes by deploying our complete internal team across marketing, tech development, compliance, legal, and finance — transforming your B-BBEE ED and SED compliance budgets into sustainable, high-growth engines that deliver commercial execution and auditable impact.

Our Methodology

The 6-Phase Venture Builder Methodology

Every startup in our programme follows a structured 6-phase journey from diagnostic onboarding through to investment-ready handover.

1. Diagnostic Onboarding Business Model Canvases and diagnostic surveys to map technical readiness, operational gaps, and growth potential.
2. Brand Positioning & Social Audit Deep social audits, keyword analysis, and rapid brand asset deployment for market visibility and trust.
3. Continuous Monitoring Real-time dashboards tracking tasks, hours, product timelines, and capacity-building metrics.
4. Route-To-Market Strategy Go-to-market channels, pricing models, distribution networks, and CRM setup for scalable customer acquisition.
5. Strategic Masterclasses Weekly founder check-ins and expert masterclasses covering legal, product, finance, compliance, and leadership.
6. Handover Readiness Investment-ready teasers, valuations, and commercial handover to corporate clients or the EPF Tech Fund.

Code Queens

South Africa's Rural STEM & AI Innovation Engine.

Empowering young women in Grade 8, 9, and 10 in underserved areas with foundational computer literacy, systems configurations, and artificial intelligence.

Code Queens is a scalable, digital-first STEM training initiative designed to equip young women in rural communities with practical digital skills and exposure to the technology-driven economy — removing geographic barriers to world-class STEM education.

60+ Schools reached
5000+ Completions
100% Digital-first delivery

Micro Enterprise Development

Empowering the Informal Economy: Sustainable Micro-Entrepreneurship.

"South Africa does not have a shortage of enterprise development programmes. It has a shortage of enterprise development programmes that survive."

We move beyond hope as an operational strategy. This initiative provides the full professional ecosystem — accredited training, mobile business infrastructure, digital tools, IoT asset protection, and mentorship — to transition citizens from dependency to active micro-enterprise scale.

Execution Framework

The 3 Critical Execution Pillars

1
Knowledge Foundation

270 beneficiaries completing accredited training in New Venture Creation, Life Skills, and Food Safety.

2
Depicted Infrastructure

135 complete Business-in-a-Box solutions: mobile food cart, gas utilities, start-up stock, tablet, and POS terminal.

3
Asset Protection via IoT

Active tracking on every mobile unit for real-time visibility, security, and operational oversight.

Programme Benefits

The Micro-Enterprise Multiplier (SROI)

Socio-Economic Ecosystem Lift

Transitioning citizens from dependency to sustainable entrepreneurship, improving household stability and localised food security.

Auditable Compliance Data

Structured, de-risked SED investments backed by clear Balanced Scorecard tracking satisfying B-BBEE requirements.

Grassroots Economic Intelligence

Real-time transaction data and regional trends to inform evidence-based policy and resource allocation.
