Why Choose Solve 4X?

At EPF, we are committed to driving meaningful change through innovation and collaboration across Africa.

Book a Scoping Call

Youth Skills Development

Equipping the next generation with the digital readiness and practical expertise to thrive.

Tech Innovation

Harnessing 4IR technologies to build scalable solutions for real-world challenges.

Sustainable Impact

Driving initiatives that create lasting, measurable value for communities.

Problem-Solving Framework

A structured, data-driven approach to identifying root causes and engineering solutions.

Collaboration for Greater Impact

Uniting public and private sectors to amplify reach and achieve shared goals.

Supporting Growth

Nurturing startups and individuals with the mentorship and resources needed to scale.

Our Approach

  • Hackathons and Ideation: We organize focused hackathons that bring together developers, entrepreneurs, and problem-solvers to brainstorm and create prototypes for key societal issues. These events foster collaboration and accelerate the development of scalable solutions.
  • Skills Gap and Market Analysis: We conduct thorough skills gap analyses and market research to identify critical areas needing attention. This ensures that our tech solutions are aligned with industry needs and effectively address the youth skills gap, driving economic growth.
  • Practical Solutions for Businesses and Communities: Solve 4X focuses on creating practical, implementable solutions that enhance business efficiency and drive community upliftment. We manage and execute projects to deliver measurable value.
