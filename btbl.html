Beyond the
Bottom Line

Beyond the Bottom Line (BTBL) is EPF's digital media platform that turns your organisation's impact into influence, through podcasts, documentaries, executive interviews, and panel discussions distributed across multiple digital channels.

Impactful work often goes unseen. Organisations invest heavily in innovation, social impact, and transformation, but struggle to convert those achievements into visibility and authority. Without a credible platform to tell the story, the impact stays invisible to the stakeholders, investors, and partners who matter most.
