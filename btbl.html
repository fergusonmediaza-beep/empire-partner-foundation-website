Transform Your Impact Into Influence.

Beyond the Bottom Line (BTBL) is EPF's strategic influence platform — turning exceptional work into national conversations through podcasts, executive interviews, documentaries, webinars and strategic storytelling.

The Visibility Gap

Most organisations are doing exceptional work — but the world does not know it. They sit in the high-impact, low-visibility quadrant: building communities, driving change, creating value. Yet their stories go untold.

BTBL creates the pathway from impact to influence — from effort to authority, from invisibility to national conversation.

Impact without visibility creates limited influence.

Six Core Content Formats

BTBL helps organisations build authority, demonstrate impact and shape national conversations.

Podcasts

Long-form conversations that position executives as sector authorities and reach professional audiences at scale.

Executive Interviews

Structured one-on-one conversations that build leadership visibility and drive thought leadership recognition.

Documentaries

Cinematic storytelling that captures real impact on the ground and brings your CSI narrative to life.

Webinars

Live engagement sessions that generate qualified leads, build community and establish your voice in key conversations.

Livestreams

Real-time broadcasting of events, panels and announcements that extend your reach beyond the room.

Strategic Storytelling

Crafted impact narratives across platforms that connect your purpose to your audience's values.
