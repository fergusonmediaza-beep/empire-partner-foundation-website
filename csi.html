Corporate Social Investment

Driving positive change and uplifting communities through technology, education, and sustainable development initiatives across Africa.

Our CSI Initiatives

Mi Desk Program

The Mi Desk initiative provides school desks to underprivileged schools across South Africa. By addressing the shortage of basic classroom resources, we aim to create better learning environments for students, empowering them to thrive academically and reach their full potential.

WiFi Sponsorship in KZN

In our effort to bridge the digital divide, EPF has sponsored WiFi installations in KwaZulu-Natal to Crystal Springs Primary School. This initiative ensures that communities have access to affordable internet, enabling them to participate in the digital economy, access educational resources, and stay connected to the world.

Donations to the NJCH

EPF has made significant contributions to the New Jerusalem Children's Home (NJCH), supporting the well-being and development of the children. Some of our key projects include:

  • Borehole Installation: Providing sustainable water access for the children's home.
  • Roof Repairs: Building a roof for the boys' dormitories, ensuring a safe and comfortable living space.
  • Donation of Computers (PiLabs): Helping equip the children with digital literacy skills by providing computers for educational use.
  • Christmas Party (2017): Hosting a memorable Christmas party for the children of NJCH, spreading joy and creating lasting memories.

Micro-Enterprise Development with CHIETA

In partnership with CHIETA, we have launched programs aimed at supporting the development of micro-enterprises. These initiatives empower local entrepreneurs with the tools, resources, and skills they need to grow their businesses, create jobs, and contribute to their communities.

Kilimanjaro Hike and Copenhagen Sponsorship

Through our sponsorship of the Kilimanjaro Hike and the stand in Copenhagen, we support Dignity Dreams, an initiative focused on raising awareness for menstrual health and providing essential sanitary products to young women in underserved communities.

By empowering women, we help drive gender equality and improve health outcomes for women and girls.

Bursaries: Investing in the Future

Empire Partner Foundation (EPF) is proud to have awarded 30 bursaries to talented and deserving students across South Africa. This initiative reflects our commitment to empowering communities through education and fostering economic growth by developing a skilled, innovative workforce.

Our bursaries provide vital financial support to students from underprivileged backgrounds, ensuring they have access to quality education, particularly in fields such as technology, entrepreneurship, and innovation.
