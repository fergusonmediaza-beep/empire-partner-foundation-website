4th Annual EPF Tech Awards: From Potential To Proof As Innovation Solves Real Systemic Challenges

By Pamela Morare · 21 April 2026

The conversation around innovation in South Africa is changing. It is no longer about what technology could achieve, but what it is already doing. The 4th Annual EPF Tech Awards, set for 12 September 2026, is at the centre of that shift, recognising individuals and organisations driving meaningful change through technology.

Over the years, the EPF Tech Awards have evolved to become South Africa's "Tech Oscars". The platform has become a national benchmark for innovation, spotlighting individuals and organisations driving meaningful change through technology.

From start-ups breaking new ground to established players redefining industries, the context has expanded considerably across sectors such as education, healthcare, fintech, and social impact.

But beyond the accolades, what the platform truly represents is growth, a maturing ecosystem where innovation is no longer theoretical but deeply embedded in everyday realities.

According to EPF CEO Joanna Govender, this year's awards mark a turning point in South Africa's innovation journey. For many young innovators, being recognised on this platform can open doors to funding, mentorship, and strategic partnerships. It creates visibility in a competitive ecosystem and builds the credibility needed to take ideas from concept to scale.

This aligns profoundly with EPF's broader mission: building practical, technology-driven solutions while empowering young people with the skills, networks, and resources needed to thrive in the digital economy.

The EPF Tech Awards capture the spirit of creating meaningful impact, bringing together the best of South Africa's tech ecosystem from all sectors and provinces, creating a space where innovation is not only showcased but strengthened through collaboration.

It is no longer defined by what could be, but by what is already being done. It is collaborative, purposeful, and increasingly impactful, driven by innovators who are not just building for scale, but building for meaning.

"The 4th Annual EPF Tech Awards will not simply celebrate excellence. It will capture a shift, from ideas to implementation, from recognition to real world change, and from potential to proof."

Joanna Govender, CEO, Empire Partner Foundation

Nominations are open now. If you are building something that matters, or know someone who is, this is the moment to put your name forward.
