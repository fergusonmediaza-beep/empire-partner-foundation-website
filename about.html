Building Dreams,
One Story at a Time.

Empire Partner Foundation (EPF) is Africa's leading youth-focused innovation ecosystem. For 10 years, we have been equipping young Africans with the skills, tools, and networks to build technology that solves real problems, from water and energy to healthcare and employment.

Join Our Mission

Who We Are

A Decade of Innovation.
Impact. Transformation.

  • For over 10 years, EPF has been at the forefront of designing and implementing innovative programmes addressing Africa's most pressing social and economic challenges.
  • Founded on the belief that sustainable development requires collaboration, innovation, and measurable impact.
  • EPF has evolved into a trusted implementation partner connecting government, corporates, development agencies, academia, technology innovators, and communities.
  • Today, EPF is an innovation ecosystem, venture builder, and implementation partner delivering technology-enabled programmes that create economic growth across Africa.

Our Journey

From Vision to Pan-African Impact

  • Our journey began with a simple but powerful vision: to bridge the gap between opportunity and access.
  • We recognised that Africa's challenges were caused by fragmented implementation, limited technology access, and disconnected ecosystems.
  • Insufficient collaboration between the public and private sectors remained one of the continent's biggest barriers to progress.
  • Today we deliver technology-enabled programmes that create economic growth, strengthen institutions, develop future-ready talent, and improve service delivery.

Why We Exist

We Exist to Solve Complex Challenges

  • We exist to solve complex challenges through collaboration, technology, and innovation.
  • Our purpose is to build inclusive ecosystems that connect ideas with implementation and innovation with impact.
  • Lasting change happens when organisations stop working in silos and co-create scalable, sustainable solutions.
  • Every programme we deliver generates measurable outcomes advancing inclusive economic growth, digital transformation, skills development, and social impact.

Our Mission

To design, implement, and scale innovative programmes that empower communities, strengthen organisations, accelerate digital transformation, and create sustainable economic opportunities across Africa.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading innovation and implementation ecosystem, enabling governments, businesses, entrepreneurs, and communities to build a more inclusive, digitally connected, and prosperous future.
