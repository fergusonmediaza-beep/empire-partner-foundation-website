Make Social Change Visible.

VSI is EPF's impact measurement platform built for corporate CSI funders, government departments, and NGOs who need verified, real-time proof of social impact. It replaces manual reporting with automated M&E, live dashboards, and transparent outcome data you can stand behind.

Request a Demo

Who is VSI For?

Corporate Investors (CSI/ESG)

Transform your Corporate Social Investment from a line item into a legacy. Track ESG scoring and visualize the ROI of your funding in real-time, ensuring compliance and maximizing brand value.

For Government & Public Sector

Monitor the deployment of critical infrastructure from water management to public safety with IoT-based smart monitoring and transparent oversight. Ensure service delivery actually reaches the communities you target.

For NGOs & Foundations

Reduce the burden of manual reporting and focus on the work that matters. Automate your outcome tracking to satisfy donors and protect your reputation as a trusted agent of change.

The Platform Capabilities

  • Real-Time Dashboards: Stop waiting for quarterly reports. Access live data streams on project status, fund utilization, and beneficiary reach across the continent.
  • Automated M&E Workflows: Replace spreadsheets with smart systems. VSI integrates directly with field technologies like digital queuing systems in clinics or smart grid integration to capture data at the source.
  • Trust & Transparency: Build unshakeable trust with stakeholders. By "making decisions based on facts" and "solid data", VSI creates an audit trail that proves your commitment to "driving meaningful change".

VSI in Action (Use Cases)

Case Study

Water & Sanitation

The Challenge: Lack of visibility into water resource allocation.

The VSI Solution: Track IoT-based leak detection and water quality sensors in real-time.

The Result

Immediate data on liters saved and communities served.

Case Study

Youth & Unemployment

The Challenge: Proving the long-term impact of training programs.

The VSI Solution: Monitor the journey of beneficiaries through the AI Academy and digital skills training.

The Result

Verifiable metrics on "youth trained" and "startups incubated."

Case Study

Public Safety

The Challenge: Assessing response times and incident management.

The VSI Solution: Aggregate data from IoT panic buttons into a central command view.

The Result

Data-backed insights to improve safety protocols and support local communities.

Why Visibility
Matters

At EPF, we believe that "we’re not just imagining a better Africa, we’re building it". But building it isn't enough; we must verify it. Visibility isn't just about charts; it's about the story of a child accessing education, a family getting clean water, and a startup getting its first investment. VSI bridges the gap between intention and proven reality.
